# Put your voice note here

1. Record 30–60 seconds on your phone. Say her name. Say why you made this.
2. Export as **MP3, mono, 64–96 kbps** (a voice memo app is fine — keep it under ~1 MB).
3. Save it as `voice/for-her.mp3` in this folder.
4. In `index.html`, set:

```js
voiceNote:'./voice/for-her.mp3',
```

The **A voice note from Joshua** button appears on the letter screen automatically.
It never autoplays — she chooses to press play.

Tips that actually matter:
- Say her name in the first three seconds.
- Do not script it perfectly. The little pauses are the good part.
- Record somewhere quiet, but do not worry about it being studio quality.
